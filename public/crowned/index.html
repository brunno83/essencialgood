The product

How is this different from minoxidil?

Minoxidil forces a response by widening blood vessels at the surface. It never touches the DHT that's strangling the root — which is why the shedding comes back the moment you stop, and why you're on it for life. It also comes with a shedding phase before anything improves. Crowned® goes after the cause instead: a copper peptide small enough to travel 4 mm down and get DHT off the follicle. Drug-free, no shedding phase, and nothing to rebound from.

I already tried a copper peptide serum and nothing happened. Why would this be different?

Almost certainly because you used one built for a face. Three things go wrong: the concentration is dosed for facial skin at facial depth, and your scalp is thicker, denser, oilier and shielded by hair. The format is built to sit on the surface — on a scalp it greases your roots and never goes down. And a copper peptide alone is one piece of four; freeing the root doesn't help if the scalp above it can't hold what grows. Crowned® was built for a scalp from the start.

What's in it — and is it safe?

Four clinically-backed ingredients and nothing else: Copper Tripeptide-1 (GHK-Cu) to release the root, niacinamide to rebuild the scalp, panthenol to stop the breakage, and Super Biotin™ at 4,000 mcg with zinc and silica to build a stronger strand. No minoxidil, no hormones, no drugs, no fragrance, no artificial dyes. Made in the USA in an FDA-registered, GMP-certified facility, and every batch is purity-tested before it ships.

Will it make my hair greasy?

No. It's a lightweight serum designed to penetrate rather than sit on the surface — which is the entire point of the format. It absorbs in seconds and doesn't need to be rinsed.

Is there a shedding phase?

No. That's a minoxidil effect, and it's one of the main reasons women stop before they ever see a result.

When will I see results?

Most women notice less shedding in the first two to four weeks — the drain, the brush and the pillow are usually the first things to change. Baby hairs along the hairline typically show up between weeks four and eight. Visible density at the part line is a months-long process, because a new strand has to grow long enough to actually cover something, and hair grows about half an inch a month. That's biology, not a flaw in the formula — which is exactly why the guarantee runs 90 days.
